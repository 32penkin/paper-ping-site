Support

Something not working, or a date that PaperPing got wrong? Write to penkin.yauhen@gmail.com or use Settings → Send feedback in the app. Please describe the problem in your own words and do not send the document itself — a made-up example with the same wording is enough.

Your documents and privacy

Where are my photos, PDFs and reminders stored?

Only on your phone, inside PaperPing. Text is recognised on the device, your documents never leave your phone, and we cannot see any of it (optional anonymous usage statistics are sent only if you allow them). On iPhone, if iCloud Backup is on, the app’s data can be part of that encrypted backup in your own Apple account. On Android it is not in the Google backup. Details: privacy policy.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in, and we keep no copy of your data on our servers.

Can I move my reminders to a new phone?

Not yet: there is no export or sync in this version. iPhone to iPhone: an iCloud (or encrypted computer) backup restored on the new phone may bring the app’s data with it. On Android the app’s data is not in the Google backup — a new phone starts empty.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Delete all data removes every reminder, photo, PDF, text and setting and cancels all scheduled notifications. Uninstalling the app removes it too. About the anonymous statistics on our side: Delete your data.

Can I lock the app?

Yes: Settings → Privacy and security → Lock with Face ID, fingerprint or passcode.

Adding things

How do I send something from another app?

Use the app’s Share button and pick PaperPing. It accepts text, a link, a picture (if you select several, the first is used) or one PDF. On iPhone, if PaperPing is not in the first row, swipe to the end and tap More to find it (you can move it to the front there). In PaperPing itself, the add button offers Take a photo, Choose from photos, Choose a file and Paste text or a link.

Does PaperPing open the links I share?

No. To keep everything on your phone it does not download the page; it reads only the text that came with the link. If the date is on the page, take a screenshot of it instead.

Which languages does it understand?

English, Polish and Russian — dates, times, amounts and phrases like “within 14 days”, “do 24 października”, “до 24 октября”. One limit for now: on Android, photos of Russian (Cyrillic) text are not recognised yet. Russian text you paste or share, and PDFs with text, work on both platforms.

Dates and suggestions

The suggested date or amount is wrong

Change it on the confirmation screen before saving — nothing is scheduled until you tap Save. Each suggestion shows the fragment of the source it came from, so you can compare. If it keeps happening with a certain kind of letter, tell us the wording (with personal details changed).

Why does it ask me for a start date?

Some deadlines are relative: “within 7 days of delivery”, “w terminie 7 dni”. Only you know when the letter arrived, so PaperPing asks instead of guessing.

It found no date at all

Save it without a date — it stays in the Inbox with the original attached — or type the date yourself. Blurry photos, handwriting and very small print are the usual reasons.

Reminders

A reminder did not arrive
  • Check that notifications are allowed for PaperPing (the app shows this at the top of Settings).
  • Focus / Do Not Disturb modes can hide notifications.
  • On Android, the system may deliver a reminder up to an hour late to save battery. It never comes early.
  • Reminders for dates without a time arrive at 09:00 by default; change it in Settings → Time for reminders without a time.
What do Done and In 1 hour do?

They open PaperPing and mark the reminder as done, or move it by one hour. In the app you can also snooze to this evening, tomorrow morning or any time.

Other

Is it free?

Yes. During the beta everything is free, with no ads.

Can PaperPing tell me whether I have to pay, or what a letter means?

No. PaperPing is not a legal or financial adviser. It finds possible actions and dates and shows where they come from; deciding what to do is up to you. Always verify important details against the original source.
