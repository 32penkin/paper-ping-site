Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

In short
  • Your photos, PDFs, screenshots, texts and reminders stay on your phone. Text is recognised on the device. We never see them.
  • There is no account. We do not know your name, email or phone number.
  • The app sends us anonymous usage statistics (which features were used, never what was in your documents). You can turn them off in Settings. We delete them after 180 days.
  • If you send feedback, we receive the message you wrote.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling of data.

2. What stays on your phone

Everything you add to PaperPing is stored only in the app’s storage on your device:

  • sources: photos, screenshots, PDFs, pasted text, links and the text that came with them;
  • text recognised from them (recognition runs on the device with the phone’s own text recognition — Apple Vision on iPhone, ML Kit on Android);
  • reminders: titles, dates, amounts, references, notes, checklists, and your settings.

We have no access to this data, and none of it is sent to us or to anyone else. PaperPing does not download the web pages behind links you share; it uses only the text that came with the link.

You can delete it at any time: delete a reminder, use Settings → Delete all data, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the data from the phone. We keep no copy on our servers. If your phone’s system backup (iCloud Backup or Google backup) is turned on, your phone may include PaperPing’s data in that encrypted backup, which Apple or Google store under your own account; we have no access to it. Deleting data in the app does not change backups made earlier — they are managed in your phone’s settings.

3. What the app sends to us

Anonymous usage statistics

To understand whether PaperPing works — for example, how many people save a second reminder, how long confirming takes, how often a suggested date has to be changed — the app sends short usage events. An event contains only:

  • the event name (for example “reminder confirmed”, “source imported”, “item completed”);
  • numbers, yes/no values and fixed categories (for example the kind of source — photo, PDF, text or link; how many seconds confirming took; whether the suggested date was kept; the detected language of the text as English, Polish, Russian or unknown);
  • a random identifier of a reminder, so that “saved” and “completed” can be counted for the same reminder;
  • a random identifier of the installation (“anonymous ID”), a session identifier, time, app version, platform (iOS or Android) and interface language.

Events never contain your texts, recognised text, images, titles, notes, amounts, currencies, dates from your documents, reference or account numbers, links, or file names. Our server accepts only a fixed list of events and values and discards anything else.

Statistics are on by default and you can turn them off at any time in Settings → Privacy and security → Anonymous usage statistics. When they are off, nothing is recorded or sent, and events waiting to be sent are deleted.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time by turning the statistics off. Retention: 180 days, then deleted automatically.

Feedback

If you use Settings → Send feedback, we receive the message you wrote together with the app version, platform, interface language and the anonymous ID. Please do not paste letters, account numbers or other personal details into it. If you write to us by email, we receive your email address and message.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering and improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Retention: as long as needed to handle the message, and no longer than 24 months — older messages are deleted automatically.

IP addresses

Like any internet connection, sending events or feedback reveals your device’s IP address to our server. We use it only to protect the service from abuse (limiting how many requests one address can send). It is kept in a short-lived counter that is deleted within 2 days, and it is not stored with the events.

5. What we do not do

  • No advertising, no advertising identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of data for marketing.
  • No profiling or automated decisions about you.
  • We do not try to find out who is behind an anonymous ID, and we do not combine feedback with usage statistics to identify anyone, except to delete data at your own request.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict its processing, to object to it, and to data portability. Write to penkin.yauhen@gmail.com; we reply within one month.

Because the statistics are anonymous to us, we can find your events only if you give us your anonymous ID, shown in Settings → About → Anonymous ID (tap “Copy”; it is also added to feedback emails). Without it we cannot tell which events are yours — they are then deleted automatically after 180 days. On iPhone the anonymous ID can survive reinstalling the app; Settings → About → Reset anonymous ID replaces it with a new random one and drops statistics not sent yet. See also How to delete your data.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Polish data protection authority: Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl, or with the authority in your EU country.

8. Children

PaperPing is meant for adults and teenagers managing their own letters and appointments. It is not directed at children under 16.

9. Changes

If the app starts sending anything new, we will update this policy before the update is released and change the date at the top. Earlier versions are available on request.
