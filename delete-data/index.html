Delete your data

PaperPing has no account, so there is nothing to close. Almost everything lives on your phone, and you delete it there. We hold only anonymous statistics and any feedback you sent.

1. On your phone — everything you added

Photos, PDFs, screenshots, texts, recognised text, reminders and settings:

  • in the app: Settings → Delete all data → Delete everything (also cancels all scheduled notifications); or
  • uninstall PaperPing.

If your phone’s system backup (iCloud Backup or Google backup) is on, older backups may still contain the app’s data; they are managed in your phone’s settings, not by us.

2. On our server — anonymous statistics

Usage events contain no content from your documents and are deleted automatically after 180 days. To stop sending them, turn off Settings → Privacy and security → Anonymous usage statistics — events not yet sent are deleted at the same moment.

To have your past events deleted sooner, email us your anonymous ID from Settings → About → Anonymous ID (tap “Copy”):

Email a deletion request

Without the ID we cannot tell which events are yours; they will still be deleted after 180 days.

3. Feedback and emails

To delete feedback you sent from the app, write to us with the approximate date (and the anonymous ID if you have it). To delete our email conversation, just ask from the same address.

How long it takes

We confirm and delete within 30 days, usually much sooner. More in the privacy policy.
