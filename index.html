How it works

The hard part of a reminder is not the notification. It is turning a letter or a long message into a reminder at all — and finding the details again when it fires.

  1. Send it

    Share a screenshot, PDF, text or link from any app, or take a photo of a paper letter.

  2. Check it

    PaperPing suggests the action and date and highlights where it found them. Fix what is off and tap Save — nothing is scheduled before that.

  3. Get it back

    At the right time the reminder arrives. Tap it and the original opens, with the date, amount or reference highlighted.

Honest about unclear dates

A Polish letter says w terminie 7 dni — within 7 days. Of what? The letter date, the day you received it? PaperPing does not guess. It flags the deadline and asks you to choose the start date.

Check this date. “Within 7 days” — from which day? Pick the date you received the letter.

English, Polish and Russian

Dates, times, amounts and deadline phrases in three languages: due by October 24, do 24 października, до 24 октября, within 14 days, w ciągu 14 dni, в течение 14 дней. The app itself is in English, Polish and Russian.

One limit for now: on Android, photos of Russian (Cyrillic) text are not recognised yet. Russian text you paste or share, links and PDFs with text work.

Made for anyone who gets letters in a language that is not their first — and for everyone else who screenshots things “for later”.

Your documents stay on your phone

Letters, bills and school messages are personal. Your documents never leave your phone. Optional anonymous usage statistics only if you allow them.

Read the privacy policy · Delete your data

  • Text is recognised on the device. Photos, PDFs, texts and reminders are never sent to our servers.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking.
  • Backups: on iPhone, if iCloud Backup is on, it may include the app’s data — encrypted, in your Apple account, not ours. On Android the app’s reminders and documents are not in the Google backup, so they do not move to a new phone.
  • Anonymous usage statistics record which features were used — never your texts, images, amounts or dates — and only if you allow them. You can change your answer in Settings.
  • Optional lock with Face ID, fingerprint or passcode. “Delete all data” wipes every reminder, document and setting on the phone in one tap and replaces the anonymous ID with a new one.
